Each fellowship meets in its own room, led by a volunteer chairperson who is a member of that fellowship.

Your first meeting takes three steps

  1. Register a username

    Pick a name, a password, and a security question in case you forget it. Adding a few numbers keeps your username unique and your identity private.

  2. Log in

    Use the same username and password on a computer or a phone. There's one login for both now.

  3. Pick a room

    Start in the Lounge to say hello, or go straight to a meeting room. You can read along without sharing.

One person shares at a time, just like a room full of chairs

Meetings here follow the same courtesy as a face-to-face meeting. Nobody interrupts. The chairperson opens the meeting, sets the topic and calls on members in turn.

  • !Raise your hand. The chair will call on you.
  • ...End a line with this to show you have more to say.
  • [done]Shows you have finished sharing.

Common questions

Do I have to share?

No. Many people read along for their first few meetings. If you want to speak, type an exclamation point and wait for the chair to call on you.

Why can't I register?

Usually the username is already taken. Choose a different name or add numbers to it, then try again. Your account must be registered before you can log in.

I forgot my password. What now?

Choose "Forgot password" on the login page and answer the security question you set when you registered.

Can I change my username?

Usernames can't be changed once registered. Ask tech support and your account will be removed so you can start over with a new name.

Can I get confirmation of my meeting attendance?

Yes. After you attend a meeting, fill out the attendance form to get a confirmation you can print or save as a PDF.

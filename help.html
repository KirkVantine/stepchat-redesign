How to log on

You register once. After that, logging in takes a username and a password.

  1. Choose Join Step Chat and open the Register tab.
  2. Pick a username. Add numbers to make sure it's unique.
  3. Create a password you'll remember, and enter your email address.
  4. Set a security question and answer, in case you forget your password.
  5. Choose Register, then log in with your new username and password.
  6. Pick a room from the list. The Lounge is open chat. Meetings are held in separate rooms.

Moving between rooms

The room list sits on the left of the chat. On a phone, tap the menu button at the top left of the chat to open it.

Changing your password

Open your account from the room list or the top of any page.

How meetings work

We try to keep each meeting as much like a face-to-face meeting as possible. Just as in a face-to-face meeting, we do not interrupt. Members take turns sharing on the topic.

A chairperson opens the meeting, chooses the topic, calls on members in turn and keeps the discussion on track. Some meetings also have greeters, who welcome latecomers with a private message.

  • !Type an exclamation point to show you'd like to speak, then wait for the chair to call on you.
  • ...Type short lines, each ending with three dots, so everyone knows you're still sharing.
  • [done]Finish with [done] so the chair knows to call on the next person.

Questions and answers

Why can't I register?

If you see "Username and password do not match", you may not have registered successfully yet. Open the Register tab before filling in the form. If the username is already taken, choose a different name or add numbers to it.

How can I change my password?

Log in, open your account, and use the "Change your password" form.

Can I change my username?

Usernames cannot be changed once they are registered. Use the tech support form below to ask for your account to be removed, so you can start over with a new name.

I forgot my password

Choose "Forgot password" on the login page. Answer the security question you set when you first registered, then choose a new password.

Can I get confirmation of my meeting attendance?

Yes. After you attend a meeting, fill out the attendance form to get a confirmation of your meeting attendance. Please check with your probation officer or caseworker to make sure that online meetings can be used to meet your court-ordered requirements.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. The chat, schedule and attendance form all work on a phone with the same login.

This is a summary. Read the full user agreement before you register.

  • Don't post anything unlawful, harmful, threatening, abusive, harassing, defamatory, vulgar or obscene.
  • You are responsible for what you post.
  • Keeping your personal anonymity is encouraged. Using a second account to get around a ban is not allowed.
  • Step Chat may edit, refuse or remove any material.
  • Opinions shared by members are their own and are not endorsed by Step Chat.
  • Personal details you share in chat can be seen and used by others, so share with care.
